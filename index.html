What I bring

To companies

An economist with a master's-level degree and more than fifteen years across economic development, credit and investment advisory, and running a business of my own. Since 2024 I also build production software: Python, FastAPI, Next.js, on-device AI. I don't hand off specs. I build the thing and ship it.

To municipalities and companies

I know your systems from the inside. Revisor Kommun prepares the municipal VAT compensation report straight from Unit4, Raindance and Visma exports. Revisor App is a complete bookkeeping system for Swedish companies, built from the ground up, and it works as a connector for Claude: you can ask your own accounts questions in plain language. Less manual work, fewer errors, same rules.

To investors and collaborators

NeuroLjus is an open-source care system for autistic people who need full support. A caregiver describes the person in plain language and the system turns it into a care protocol a care robot can follow. It runs on the device, so sensitive data never leaves the home. It comes from years of working with people with special needs, and it is built to run anywhere in the world. I am looking for people who want to build it with me.
