§ 02Research

Economic models whose structure is explicit and whose forecast claims survive a test that is correctly sized.

Economic Evolution Lab

A reproducible library of macroeconomic models: a real business cycle model and a three-equation New Keynesian DSGE. Equilibrium conditions are written symbolically and solved to first order with the generalized Schur (QZ) method of Klein (2000), with determinacy checked by the Blanchard-Kahn condition. Rational expectations and adaptive learning can be swapped inside the same model.

The standard Clark-West test, applied to fixed versus constant-gain learning forecasts, rejects a true null about 60% of the time instead of 5%. The library calibrates it by Monte Carlo. Under the corrected test, the 6.2% gain in one-quarter-ahead US inflation forecasts over 1985 to 2019 is not statistically significant.

Applied economics

01 – 03

01

Revisor App

Swedish bookkeeping law, written as tested code.

Live product
Problem
Small Swedish companies need bookkeeping that follows Swedish accounting and VAT rules without hiring an accountant for every question.
Method
Double-entry validation in exact decimal arithmetic, the full SKV 4700 VAT return mapping, SIE 4 export, and Skatteverket's 2026 tax tables imported with checksummed provenance. The Claude connector is read-only.
Evidence
About 465 automated tests run on every change.
Limit
VAT filing produces a signed draft; direct submission to Skatteverket is not live yet.
Technical noterevisorapp.se
02

Revisor Kommun

Municipal VAT compensation, checked rule by rule against the law.

Pilot
Problem
Municipalities can leave VAT compensation unclaimed, or claim it wrongly, under the law on VAT compensation for municipalities (LEMK, SFS 2005:807).
Method
Six rule-based checks grounded in LEMK, its ordinance (SFS 2005:811) and case law such as HFD 2013:79 and HFD 2016:51. Each finding cites its legal source and a confidence level. A ledger in whole öre adds period locks and reconciliation against the municipality's own balances.
Evidence
31 test files for the municipal module and a 15-item evidence checklist for the SKV 5933 claim.
Limit
Not yet run on a real municipal export. Amounts on the claim form are never filled in automatically.
Technical notePilot
03

Nicho Radar

Where Nordic public money buys the same software again and again.

Prototype
Problem
Which software needs do many separate Nordic public buyers procure repeatedly?
Method
597 notices from TED, the EU's public procurement journal, for software and IT services (CPV 48 and 72) in Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland. Notices are grouped along the CPV code hierarchy, and a niche counts when at least two different buyers procure it.
Evidence
56 niches covering 566 of the 597 notices; six automated tests.
Limit
A four-week snapshot (26 August to 23 September 2026), and the largest groups are broad CPV categories.

Care and cognition

04 – 05

04

NeuroLjus

A care protocol a caregiver can write in plain words, with safety enforced in code.

Research prototype
Problem
Autistic people who need full support often cannot say what they need, and their caregivers carry that work alone.
Method
A language model only proposes settings inside a strict schema. A deterministic, tested planner turns them into a portable care protocol. Local risk escalation, safety limits the model cannot lower and a caregiver who starts and stops every routine are enforced in code. The camera layer runs on the device and measures movement, not emotions.
Evidence
A library of 1,710 open-access studies with DOIs checked in Crossref and retracted papers excluded, 10 crisis protocols that cite it, 146 automated tests, and a published validation plan.
Limit
No physical robot is connected yet, and the signals have not been validated with people.
Technical noteneuroljus.comSource code
05

Neuromesure

Thinking time, measured with a taxonomy from cognitive science.

Prototype
Problem
People have no simple way to see how their thinking time is spent, and whether it leads anywhere.
Method
Free-text thought reports are labelled by a language model with a taxonomy that includes directed, free and stuck thought (Christoff et al., 2016). Metrics are descriptive and time-weighted, with minimum sample sizes and "coincides, not causes" wording.
Evidence
94 unit tests, and 92% label stability across repeated runs on a 12-item test set.
Limit
Not yet a validated psychometric instrument. That needs expert-labelled data and reliability testing.
Technical noteneuromesure.com

Public interest

06 – 07

06

Ojo al Giro

Promised earthquake aid, each figure traced to its source.

Live register
Problem
After the earthquake in Colombia on 10 August 2026, announcements of aid were hard to follow and to hold to account.
Method
A hand-curated register where every row links to its source. It separates announced, disbursed and delivered aid, prevents double counting and credits shown as donations, and converts currencies only at cited, dated rates. It can draft a formal information request under Colombian law (Ley 1755 de 2015).
Evidence
58 sourced rows, checked on every change by a validator with 18 rules and 19 tests.
Limit
It tracks announcements, not delivery to households.
Technical noteLive registerSource code
07

Bunker Now

The nearest shelter, in your language, in one tap.

Live app
Problem
About two million people in Sweden were born abroad, and many do not know where the nearest shelter is or what the alarms mean.
Method
The nearest of 62,739 active public shelters from the register of Myndigheten för civilt försvar, by great-circle distance. If it is more than five minutes away, the app first says to take cover where you are. Seven languages, and offline search in the Android app.
Evidence
98.8% of shelters matched to Lantmäteriet street addresses; built in one day at Claude Build Day Stockholm and updated with the October 2026 register.
Limit
Travel times are straight-line estimates, and translations still await native-speaker review.
Technical noteLive appSource code
