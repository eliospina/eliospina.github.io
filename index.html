Economic Evolution Lab

A reproducible library of macroeconomic models: a real business cycle model and a three-equation New Keynesian DSGE. The equilibrium conditions are written symbolically and solved to first order with the generalized Schur (QZ) method of Klein (2000), with determinacy checked by the Blanchard-Kahn condition. Rational expectations and adaptive learning can be swapped inside the same model.

The main finding is methodological. Comparing a fixed forecast with a constant-gain learning forecast using the standard Clark-West test rejects a true null about 60% of the time instead of 5%. The library calibrates the test by Monte Carlo, and under the corrected test the 6.2% gain in one-quarter-ahead US inflation forecasts over 1985 to 2019 is not statistically significant.

Applied economics

Revisor App

Live product
Problem
Small Swedish companies need bookkeeping that follows Swedish accounting and VAT rules without hiring an accountant for every question.
Method
Swedish rules encoded as deterministic, tested code: double-entry validation in exact decimal arithmetic, the full SKV 4700 VAT return mapping, SIE 4 export, and 2026 tax tables imported from Skatteverket with checksummed provenance. The Claude connector is read-only.
Evidence
About 465 automated tests run in continuous integration.
Limit
VAT filing produces a signed draft; direct submission to Skatteverket is not live yet.

Revisor Kommun

Pilot
Problem
Municipalities can leave VAT compensation unclaimed, or claim it wrongly, under the law on VAT compensation for municipalities (LEMK, SFS 2005:807).
Method
Six rule-based checks grounded in LEMK, its ordinance (SFS 2005:811) and case law such as HFD 2013:79 and HFD 2016:51. Each finding cites its legal source and a confidence level. A ledger in whole öre adds period locks and reconciliation against the municipality's own balances.
Evidence
31 test files for the municipal module and a 15-item evidence checklist for the SKV 5933 claim.
Limit
Not yet run on a real municipal export. Amounts on the claim form are never filled in automatically.

Nicho Radar

Prototype
Problem
Which software needs do many separate Nordic public buyers procure again and again?
Method
597 notices from TED, the EU's public procurement journal, for software and IT services (CPV 48 and 72) in Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland. Notices are grouped along the CPV code hierarchy, and a niche counts when at least two different buyers procure it.
Evidence
56 niches covering 566 of the 597 notices; six automated tests.
Limit
A four-week snapshot (26 August to 23 September 2026), and the largest groups are broad CPV categories.

Care and cognition

NeuroLjus

Research prototype
Problem
Autistic people who need full support often cannot say what they need, and their caregivers carry that work alone.
Method
A language model only proposes settings inside a strict schema. A deterministic, tested planner turns them into a portable care protocol. Safety rules are enforced in code: local risk escalation, safety limits the model cannot lower, and a caregiver who starts and stops every routine. The camera layer runs on the device and measures movement, not emotions.
Evidence
A library of 1,710 open-access studies with DOIs checked in Crossref and retracted papers excluded, 10 crisis protocols that cite it, 146 automated tests, and a published validation plan.
Limit
No physical robot is connected yet, and the signals have not been validated with people.

Neuromesure

Prototype
Problem
People have no simple way to see how their thinking time is spent, and whether it leads anywhere.
Method
Free-text thought reports are labelled by a language model with a taxonomy drawn from cognitive science, including directed, free and stuck thought (Christoff et al., 2016). Metrics are descriptive and time-weighted, with minimum sample sizes and "coincides, not causes" wording.
Evidence
94 unit tests, and 92% label stability across repeated runs on a 12-item test set.
Limit
Not yet a validated psychometric instrument. That needs expert-labelled data and reliability testing.

Public interest

Ojo al Giro

Live register
Problem
After the earthquake in Colombia on 10 August 2026, announcements of aid were hard to follow and to hold to account.
Method
A hand-curated register where every row links to its source. It separates announced, disbursed and delivered aid, prevents double counting and credits shown as donations, and converts currencies only at cited, dated rates. It can draft a formal information request under Colombian law (Ley 1755 de 2015).
Evidence
58 sourced rows, checked on every change by a validator with 18 rules and 19 tests.
Limit
It tracks announcements, not delivery to households.

Bunker Now

Live app
Problem
About two million people in Sweden were born abroad, and many do not know where the nearest shelter is or what the alarms mean.
Method
The nearest of 62,739 active public shelters from the register of Myndigheten för civilt försvar, by great-circle distance, with 98.8% matched to Lantmäteriet street addresses. If the shelter is more than five minutes away, the app first says to take cover where you are. Seven languages, and offline search in the Android app.
Evidence
Built in one day at Claude Build Day Stockholm, September 2026, and kept up to date with the October 2026 register.
Limit
Travel times are straight-line estimates, and translations still await native-speaker review.

What I bring

To companies and municipalities

I build software that works inside your rules. Revisor Kommun reviews municipal VAT compensation from the general-ledger exports municipalities already produce, as CSV or SIE files. Revisor App is a bookkeeping system for Swedish companies, and it works as a read-only connector for Claude: you can ask your own accounts questions in plain language. Behind it is an economist with a master's-level degree and more than fifteen years across economic development, credit and investment advisory, and running a business of my own.

To investors and collaborators

NeuroLjus is an open-source care system for autistic people who need full support. A caregiver describes the person in plain language, and the system turns it into a structured care protocol: today it runs on a phone, and it is specified so a care robot can follow it. Camera signals stay on the device. I presented it on the main stage at Claude Founder House Stockholm in October 2026, and I am looking for people who want to build it with me.
